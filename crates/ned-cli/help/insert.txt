insert before|after|start|end [all] SEL TEXT

Inserts TEXT next to each span of SEL (before, after) or inside it, at its
start or end. On a syntax item, start and end mean its .body. Text for a
whole-line span is line-oriented and re-indented; text for a partial span is
inserted verbatim. An empty body like `fn f() {}` is opened onto new lines.
Leading or trailing blank lines in TEXT are kept, to separate items.

Examples:
    insert after import:std::fmt "use std::io;"
    insert end impl:Parser <<END

    fn reset(&mut self) {
        self.pos = 0;
    }
    END
