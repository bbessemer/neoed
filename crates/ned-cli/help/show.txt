show [SEL [+N]]

Prints the lines holding each span of SEL, headed PATH:START-END, as N:text.
Without SEL, prints every file in the set. +N adds N lines of context before
and after each span. Nearby regions merge. Output always shows the original
text, even after edits earlier in the script.

Examples:
    show fn:parse
    show /TODO/ +3
    show all /TODO/
    show 10-30
