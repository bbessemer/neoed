show [SEL [+N]]

Prints the lines holding each span of SEL, headed PATH:START-END, as N:text.
Without SEL, prints every file in the set. +N adds N lines of context before
and after each span. Nearby regions merge. Output shows the text as of the
start of its stage: the original, unless the script has a `|` before it.
`show all /re/` is a search: no match prints `no matches for /re/ in N files`
instead of failing.
A line range alone that ends past a file's last line shows up to it, with a
note.

Examples:
    show fn:parse
    show /TODO/ +3
    show all /TODO/
    show 10-30
