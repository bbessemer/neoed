text

TEXT is a string, "..." on one line, or a heredoc:
  <<END      body on the following lines, up to a line that is just END.
             Re-based: its common indentation is stripped, and each level
             becomes one indent unit of the file (tabs or spaces).
  <<'END'    verbatim.
Line-oriented text (for whole-line spans) gets a final newline and is
indented to the target: the span's first line for replace/before/after, the
first line inside the span for start/end. Several heredocs on one line take
their bodies in order. Write the text at column 0; ned indents it.
