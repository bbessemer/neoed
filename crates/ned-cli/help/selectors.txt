selectors

Lines: 12, 12-20, 12-$, $ (last line).
Regex: /re/ with flags i, s. Literal: "text" (escapes \n \t \" \\).
Block: <<END heredoc matches whole lines, ignoring indentation.
Syntax: kind:name, * a wildcard (fn alone is fn:*); quote others: import:"a.b".
  Kinds: fn class struct enum variant trait interface impl type const var
  field mod import, and in Markdown section item table code (which depend on
  the language; outline shows them). section:"Intro" is a # heading and its
  content; its .body is the content after the heading, subsections included.
  impl:Name is the impl's self type; a trait impl is also impl:"Trait for Name".
  A Go method is fn:"Recv.Name", and fn:Name matches every Name.
  A syntax item includes its doc comments and attributes (decorators, and in
  JS/TS its export); a Python item's .doc is its docstring, and its .body the
  lines after it. In JS/TS, const f = () => {} is both fn:f and const:f.
Parts: .body .params (inside delimiters) .name .sig .doc .attrs .ret .type
  .value .whole (with docs); .lines splits into lines (fn:f>12 picks one).
  Via the language server, on any step: .refs (each use), .def (the defining
  item): all fn:parse.refs, "helper(".def. With -w they reach every file.
Nest with > (no spaces): impl:Parser>fn:new>"x". Scope: 40-80>fn:new,
file:src/a.rs>fn:new. Raw: query{(node) @sel}.
Range: A..B, whole lines from a match of A to the next match of B:
/^## 6/../^## 7/, fn:a..fn:c.
Ambiguous matches are errors listing selectors to paste; `all SEL` takes every
match.
