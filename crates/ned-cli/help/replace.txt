replace [all] SEL with TEXT

Replaces each span of SEL with TEXT. A whole-line span (a syntax item, a line
range, a heredoc match) takes TEXT as whole lines, re-indented to the span's
first line; empty TEXT removes them, as delete does. A partial span, such as a
string match, takes TEXT verbatim.
A field or variant keeps its trailing `,`, and a syntax item its doc comments
and attributes unless TEXT starts with its own. `$` is never expanded; use
`sub`. With a pattern in SEL, @name in TEXT is its capture (ned help patterns).

Examples:
    replace fn:parse.params with "&mut self, depth: usize"
    replace fn:parse.body with <<END
    let tok = self.next()?;
    self.parse_expr(tok)
    END
