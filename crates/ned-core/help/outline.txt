outline [SEL]

Lists each file's syntax items as `START-END kind:name`, indented by nesting,
so a line can be pasted back as a selector. Imports collapse to one line.
Items inside functions are left out; fields and variants appear only when SEL
is their parent. With SEL, lists the items inside each span of SEL.
Files without a language are skipped{cli: (use --lang)}{repl: (start the REPL with --lang)}.

Examples:
    outline
    outline impl:Parser
    outline struct:Config
