delete [all] SEL

Removes each span of SEL. A whole-line span removes its full lines and tidies
the blank lines beside them: between two other lines, only as many stay as the
larger of the gaps above and below the span; next to a bracket or after a line
ending in `:` (Python), only those already there; at the start or end of the
file, none.

Examples:
    delete fn:debug_dump
    delete all fn:test_*
    delete impl:Parser>"#[inline] "
