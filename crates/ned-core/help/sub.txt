sub [[all] SEL] /re/ with TEXT

Replaces every match of the regex inside each span of SEL, or of every file in
the set. In TEXT, $1, ${name} and $0 expand to captures and $$ is a literal $;
${1}x is group 1 then x ($1x is group 1x). A group the regex lacks is an error.
The replacement is verbatim. No match at all is an error. Regexes use Rust
syntax; ^ and $ match at line boundaries (once on each line of a span); flags: i
(ignore case), s (. matches newline).

Examples:
    sub /\bold_name\b/ with "new_name"
    sub fn:parse /tok(\d)/ with "token$1"
    file src/*.rs; sub /Result<(\w+), Error>/ with "Result<$1>"
