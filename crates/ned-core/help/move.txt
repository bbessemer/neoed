move [all] SEL before|after|start|end DEST

Removes each span of SEL, as `delete` does, and inserts its text at DEST, as
`insert` does: re-indented, and inside the .body for start/end on a syntax
item. DEST must be one span, possibly in another file of the set. With `all`,
spans arrive in their original order. A moved item that had a blank line
beside it is separated for before/after by as many blank lines as DEST has
beside it, or else as it had at its source.

Examples:
    move fn:helper after fn:main
    move all fn:test_* end mod:tests
    file src/a.rs src/b.rs; move fn:util end file:src/b.rs>impl:B
